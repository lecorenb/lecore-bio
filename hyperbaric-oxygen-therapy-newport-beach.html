Questions

HBOT Questions, Answered

How long is a session?

Standard sessions are a full 75 minutes in our 2.0 ATA hard-shell chamber. Premium members can also use 30-minute sessions as part of their monthly plan.

What does a session feel like?

You sit in a comfortable reclined seat while the chamber gently pressurizes. Most people read, rest, or listen to music, then walk straight back into their day.

How much does it cost?

Your first session is $100. After that, sessions are $240, or choose our 8 and 16 session packages. Members pay $100 on Access and $80 on Premium and Elite, and Elite includes 4 sessions every month.

Can I combine HBOT with other therapies?

Yes. Many clients pair HBOT with red light therapy and PEMF. The $95 LeCore Reset lets you try all three, plus a Nervous System & Recovery Scan, in one 90-minute visit.

Can I use HSA, FSA, or financing?

Yes. We accept HSA and FSA cards, and you can pay over 6 months at 0% APR with Cherry or CareCredit, subject to approval.
