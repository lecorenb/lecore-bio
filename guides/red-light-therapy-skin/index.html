Why Your Skin Loses Its Glow

As we age, the cells in our skin produce less energy. Less energy means slower repair, less collagen, and skin that looks tired, thinner and less firm. Sun exposure, stress and poor sleep speed this up.

The good news: you can give your skin cells the energy they need to act younger again.

How Red Light Works

Inside every skin cell are tiny power plants called mitochondria. Within them is an enzyme called cytochrome C oxidase, which helps turn oxygen into usable energy.

1

Light In

Red and near infrared wavelengths reach your skin cells.

2

Energy Up

Cytochrome C oxidase absorbs the light and cells make more energy.

3

Skin Rebuilds

Collagen and elastin production ramps up for firmer skin.

When your cells get that boost, they:

  • Produce more energy
  • Repair themselves more efficiently
  • Ramp up collagen and elastin production
  • Calm inflammation and redness

No heat damage. No downtime. Just light your skin knows how to use.

Sessions are short, comfortable and relaxing. Most people describe it as the easiest part of their week.

Is Red Light Right for You?

Red light therapy is safe for most skin types and ages. If you are pregnant, take photosensitizing medications, or have a history of skin cancer, talk with us before starting.

Common Questions

Does red light therapy hurt?

No. It feels like gentle warmth. Most people relax or close their eyes during the session.

Is there any downtime?

None. You can go straight back to work, the gym or dinner.

How soon will I see results?

Most people see calmer, brighter skin within 1 to 2 weeks. Firming builds over 8 to 12 weeks.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. Red light pairs well with facials, injectables and post procedure recovery. Ask us how to time it.
