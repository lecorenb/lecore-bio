Our approach

Gentle, Specific Care

Webster Technique

Balance for Your Pelvis

The Webster Technique is a specific prenatal analysis and gentle adjustment focused on the pelvis and sacrum. It helps reduce tension in the pelvis and the ligaments around it, so you can move more comfortably as your body changes.

Dr. Navid is Webster certified

Soft tissue work

Release Tight Hips and Back

Gentle hands-on work releases tight hips, low back muscles, and round ligaments, so your adjustments feel easier and your relief lasts longer.

Questions

Prenatal Questions, Answered

When can I start prenatal care?

Any time during pregnancy. Many moms start in the first or second trimester, and it is never too late to begin in the third.

What happens at my first visit?

Your 60-minute first visit includes a Webster-specific exam, your first gentle adjustment, and soft tissue work, plus a plan for the rest of your pregnancy and postpartum.

How often should I come in?

Many moms come in weekly, especially in the third trimester. We will recommend a schedule that fits your body and your stage of pregnancy.

How much does it cost?

Your first visit is $95 for a limited time, regularly $195. Follow-up visits are $95 and take about 30 minutes.

Do you offer postpartum care?

Yes. We help you transition smoothly after delivery with gentle adjustments, soft tissue work, and recovery-focused red light and PEMF therapy.

Should I talk to my OB or midwife first?

We are happy to work alongside your OB or midwife. If you have any questions about your pregnancy, check with them too.

Can I use HSA or FSA?

Yes. We accept HSA and FSA cards.
